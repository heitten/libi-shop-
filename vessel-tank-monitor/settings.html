Each tank uses its 100% capacity (m³) to convert a reading into volume/weight. By default, daily entry uses % Full (no extra setup needed). If you know a tank's Max Sounding depth (cm) — the sounding reading when the tank is completely full — enter it below to enable accurate cm-based entry for that tank. For tanks with a non-rectangular shape (peak tanks, sloped double-bottoms), add extra calibration points (sounding cm → volume m³) from your sounding tables for a precise curve instead of a straight line.
